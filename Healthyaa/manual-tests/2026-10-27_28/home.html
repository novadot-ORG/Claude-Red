FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

"Answers to common queries for your peace of mind"

01. What types of blood tests does Healthyaa offer?

Healthyaa offers a wide range of blood tests, categorized into individual tests and packages. Individual tests cover specific markers like blood glucose or ESR, while packages combine multiple tests for a comprehensive health assessment. Examples include the Essential Blood Test, Comprehensive Test, and Advance Blood Test, each tailored to different needs and budgets.

02. Which test should I take?

Most of our tests are clearly labeled, making it easy for you to determine if they are suitable for your needs.

If you’re looking for a more comprehensive assessment of your health, we recommend testing our packages based tests.

03. Do I need to prepare for my test?

If the test you choose requires fasting (abstaining from eating or drinking anything except water for 10-14 hours before the sample collection), it will be clearly mentioned in your confirmation email. Some hormonal tests may need to be performed on specific days, and this information will be provided in the test details and confirmation email.

In general, we recommend staying hydrated by drinking plenty of water before your appointment. Additionally, keeping your arm warm and wearing loose-fitting clothing will make it easier for the nurse to access your arm.

04. Where do I go to have my blood sample taken?

You can book your sample collection at the Healthyaa Leicester clinic or choose from 400+ partner clinics across the UK. If you book at our Leicester clinic, there is no separate phlebotomy charge. After selecting your test, choose the most convenient clinic and follow the booking instructions in your confirmation email.

05. What will happen when I have my sample taken?

The nurse will draw a small amount of blood from your arm. If you’re feeling nervous, don’t hesitate to communicate your concerns with the nurse, and they will do their best to make you comfortable.

Certain tests may require additional information such as your height, weight, and blood pressure. The nurse will collect these measurements and complete a health questionnaire with any relevant information pertaining to your test.

Your test results will be processed by our partner labs and delivered to your registered email address.

06. Is Healthyaa Laboratory accredited?

We collaborate with independent labs that are registered with the Care Quality Commission (CQC). Our partner labs adhere to ISO laboratory standards and employ various monitoring techniques to ensure the reliability of the test results they produce.

Certain tests may require additional information such as your height, weight, and blood pressure. The nurse will collect these measurements and complete a health questionnaire with any relevant information pertaining to your test.

Your test results will be processed by our partner labs and delivered to your registered email address.

07. How to access the reports?

You can expect to receive your reports in your registered email within 24 hours of sample collection. Please check the spam folder too else contact us.

08. How can I contact Healthyaa for further questions or assistance?

You can reach Healthyaa by phone at 0116 467 0722 or by email at book@healthyaa.co.uk. Their customer support team is available to answer your questions and provide guidance. You can also find more information on their website, including blog articles on various health topics.
